## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## 
HIO CN2 - SERIAL DATA CABLE - wired to MC34050 chip 
HIO CN2 - PINOUT (FROM BACK (TRACE SIDE) OF BOARD )

1 - MC3450 Pin 11  - DR2 Out (active low) 
2 - MC3450 Pin 10  - DR2 Out 
3 - MC3450 Pin 6   - Rec2 In +
4 - MC3450 Pin 7   - Rec2 In - 

## ## ## ## 
# HIO CN2 to DIF42 - other end of the cable 
  DIF42 - PINOUT (FROM BACK (TRACE SIDE) OF BOARD )

1 - GND
2 - MC3450 Pin 1   - REC1 In- 
3 - MC3450 Pin 2   - REC1 In+  ( pulled LOW through R72 )
4 - MC3450 Pin 14  - DR1 out 
5 - MC3450 Pin 13  - DR1 out (active low) 
6 - GND


DATA IN TO DIF    = MC3450 Pin 3  
DATA OUT FROM DIF = MC3450 Pin 15 (pulled LOW)







## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## 
CN3 - ANALOG PRESSURE SENSOR 
CN3 - PINOUT (FROM BACK (TRACE SIDE) OF BOARD )

1 - GND 
2 - 12V               (schematics say 12V on page 11/25 )
3 - pressure sensor 1 (analog signal)
4 - pressure sensor 2 (analog signal)
5 - pressure sensor 3 (analog signal)
6 - breakout to CN4 - (probably pressure sensor 4 (analog signal))
