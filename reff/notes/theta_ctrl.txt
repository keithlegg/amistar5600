controls theta for heads 1&2

# white stripe wires are GND 
# solid are controls  ( PWM CC/CCW ) 

- YW--> GND (GW/YW wires joined)
- Y --> H2 PWM CCW
- GW--> GND
- G --> H2 PWM CC

- BW--> GND (RW/BW wires joined)
- B --> H1 PWM CCW
- RW--> GND 
- R --> H1 PWM CC
